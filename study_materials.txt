--- MOCK TEST 11 ---
Part 1.1 (No preparation)[cite: 11]
* What is your favourite restaurant? (Sample Answer: A little Italian place downtown is my favourite place to eat. I've never had better pizza or pasta. The staff is very helpful, and the place feels cosy.)[cite: 11]
* Do you like going to the beach? (Sample Answer: I do enjoy beach time. It makes me feel so calm to hear the waves and touch the sand between my toes. I also like to swim and play beach volleyball with friends.)[cite: 11]
* What is your favourite subject at school? (Sample Answer: History is my favourite subject in school. I find it very interesting to learn about other societies and past events.)[cite: 11]

Part 1.2 (Free time)[cite: 12]
* Compare the first picture (reading in a library) to the second (camping with friends).[cite: 12]
* How do you usually spend your free time?[cite: 12]
* What are the pluses and minuses of spending free time on these ways? (Sample Point: Reading is great to keep your mind sharp, but can be lonely. Camping gets you closer to nature, but depends on weather.)[cite: 12]

Part 2 (1 min preparation)[cite: 13]
* Discuss a personal goal you have set for yourself. (Sample: Learn Spanish to travel and grow as a person.)[cite: 13]
* What steps are you taking to achieve this goal? (Sample: Signed up for classes, using apps like Duolingo, watching Spanish films.)[cite: 13]
* Why is setting personal goals important for personal development? (Sample: Gives direction and purpose, helps focus efforts.)[cite: 13]

Part 3 (Debate)[cite: 14]
Topic: School uniforms should be mandatory in all schools.[cite: 14]
* Pros: Promotes equality and reduces socio-economic disparities, simplifies dress codes and maintains discipline, fosters a sense of unity.[cite: 14]
* Cons: Infringes on students' freedom of expression, may be uncomfortable or costly, limits creativity.[cite: 14]
